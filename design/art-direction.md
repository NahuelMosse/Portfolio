# Art direction: the chapter illustrations

Every illustration on the page follows this line. It applies to a regenerated image, a new chapter, and any tool (Codex, Gemini, Kling).

## The line in one sentence

A screen-printed noir comic: three flat inks on rough paper. The shapes are carved out of solid black by one hard light. Each frame tells the one act of its chapter, and in a project that act is him using the real product.

## Inks: three, flat

| Ink | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink black | `#0E0C0B` | at least 55% of the frame, in solid masses |
| Bone | `#E9DFC9` | the light: faces, hands, paper, the lit edge of objects |
| Spot color | per chapter, below | one object or one light per frame, the one that carries the chapter's meaning, at most 10% of the area |

- There are no grays and no gradients. A midtone is drawn as halftone dots of black on bone, or as ink hatching.
- The spot color is printed as its own plate, 1 to 2 px off register from the black, so it reads as print.

**Spot color per chapter.** In a project it is the product's own color, so the art and the interface are the same world. Everywhere else it is the site's crimson.

| Chapter | Spot color | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open, experience, offer, closing, employers | `#B3261E` crimson | the site |
| The dev kit | `#B3261E` crimson | the kit is the site's own method |
| Grit | `#1F55D6` blue | the app's primary |
| Guardia | `#36D1A8` teal | the TRIAJE logotype |
| Cebador | `#F0A8AC` pink | the site's background |
| ClubNahuelMosse | `#CDEB3A` lime | the app's accent |

## Mark

- **Brush contour.** One brush line, thick on the outer contour and thin inside.
- **Shading.** Shadows are closed black shapes, not soft falloff. The texture is dry brush at the end of strokes, spatter only where something is hit.
- **Paper.** The bone carries a fine paper tooth.
- **Detail.** Detail is concentrated where the eye goes (hands, face, the device, the spot-color object). The rest is reduced to silhouettes.

## Light and camera

- **Light.** One hard light source per frame, and its shadow shapes the composition. In a project the device's screen can be that light: its glow is the spot color, drawn as flat shapes.
- **No atmosphere.** No glow, bloom, haze or dust in the beam, and no lens effects.
- **Shots.** Each frame uses a different shot, chosen for its act: an extreme close-up of hands, top-down, a low angle, over the shoulder, a silhouette against the light, a wide shot with him small.
- **Composition.** Asymmetric. Never a centered portrait looking at the camera, except the cold open.
- **Room for type.** One side or band of the frame stays solid black, where the page sets the chapter's title.

## The device in a project frame

Only the cold open lets the art lead. In a project the real interface leads: the page crops the frame around the drawn device and puts the real interface in its screen, so what shows of the art is the hand, the bezel and the nearest objects framing the work. The device is drawn to receive it:

- **Placement.** The device faces the viewer front-on and flat, its screen an axis-aligned rectangle with no perspective, tilt, glare or reflection.
- **Size.** The device is the largest thing in the frame, closer to the camera than he is, so the interface reads:

  | Device | 16:9 | 4:5 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Phone screen | at least 60% of the frame's height | at least 45% of the frame's width |
  | Laptop or monitor screen | at least 45% of the frame's width | at least 80% of the frame's width |

  A phone is held by his hand in the foreground, with his face beyond it. A laptop or monitor is in the near foreground, with him behind or beside it.
- **The screen.** It is filled with pure key green `#00FF00`, edge to edge inside the bezel. Nothing overlaps it, not a finger, not a cable.
- **The bezel.** It is drawn in the ink like everything else.
- **The device type.** The device matches the product: a phone for an app, a laptop or monitor for a website or a browser game.

## Him

- **Likeness.** He is the only figure: late twenties, dark curly hair, a thin face, a plain black t-shirt.
- **Drawing.** He is drawn with the same economy as everything else. His face is half in shadow, with no idealized skin and no glamour.
- **Action.** He is doing the act of the chapter, not posing.

## Never

- Wet reflective floors, rain, puddles.
- Neon, smoke, volumetric rays, bokeh, particles.
- Smooth airbrushed shading, 3D rendering, a photographic look.
- A spot color sprayed around as decoration, or two spot colors in one frame.
- Text, letters or numbers anywhere in the image.
- A drawn interface on a project device: its screen is key green, because the page shows the real one.
- Extra fingers, warped hands, a second person.
