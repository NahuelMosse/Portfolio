# Scene media

Every illustration is made by the requester in Gemini (gemini.google.com, free, image model "Nano Banana") from the prompt below, saved here as `public/film/<id>.webp` (the 4:5 and 16:9 sizes in the id). The two cold open loops are made in Kling AI (klingai.com, free daily credits, image to video). The page reads each file when it exists; a file that is not delivered is a declared gap, shown as a graded dark field, never a stand-in.

Style shared by every Gemini prompt: one continuous dark Baroque fresco of a pantheon, every image another stretch of the same painting; classical oil-painting and marble figures crowned, veiled or armored, set flat against black or deep red, four-point stars, halftone print, cracks and film grain, no frames and no architecture. Strict palette: near-black, bone cream and one deep crimson red. No text, no logos, no watermark.

Real pieces (screens, captures, photos) are not generated: they are listed in the last section.
